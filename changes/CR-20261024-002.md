# Change Request: CR-20261024-002

## 1. Objective

Deliver the complete product & pitch-readiness upgrade requested by Quốc Thanh and Nguyễn Ngọc Khánh Phương for VSIC 2026, R&D to Startup 2026, Panasonic Green Impact, and HITC Innostar:
1. **Visual 4-Step Operational Process Flow (`Quy Trình Chuyển Đổi Số 4 Bước`)**: Show SMEs and investors the exact operational transition from Manual Excel & Manual District Splitting -> Automated Clustering & QĐ 23/2018 Protection -> Driver Execution & Backhaul Matching -> Automated ESG/Carbon Reporting.
2. **Empty-Run (`Chạy Xe Rỗng`) & Backhaul Optimization**: Compute and visualize stem/return deadhead distance (`km chạy rỗng`), empty-running rate (`Tỷ lệ chạy rỗng %`), and return-trip backhaul pickup matching (`Đơn gom hàng chiều về kho`) across all 4 routing strategies—proving how EcoMiles cuts empty running from `~31.8%` down to `~11.4%`.
3. **Enterprise Multi-Tenant Data Security, Privacy & Access Approval Console (`Bảo Mật Đa Doanh Nghiệp & Phê Duyệt Quyền Truy Cập`)**: Address the VSIC mentor's security question head-on with an interactive Tenant Isolation & Data Governance module (Zero-Retention RAM mode vs. Isolated Enterprise Workspace, NĐ 13/2023/NĐ-CP PII masking `090***1234`, SHA-256 dataset integrity hash, interactive Admin Access Approval toggle, and 1-Click Data Purge).
4. **Automated Report Generation Studio (`Trung Tâm Xuất Báo Cáo Kiểm Toán Tự Động`)**: Prove live on the dashboard how raw dispatch data automatically compiles into a multi-sheet `.xlsx` workbook (`Tong_Hop_ESG_GLEC`, `Chi_Tiet_Tuyen_Xe`, `Kiem_Toan_Don_Hang`), `.csv`, and printable Executive Certificate PDF with 1 click.
5. **Full-Site Humanizer & Em-Dash (`—`) Removal**: Remove all `—` em-dashes, AI-slop phrasing, and internal developer jargon (`Act I..IV`, `Proxy`, `Ephemeral`, `GLX-HDT-v1`, `Zero-Storage`) across every component in `apps/landing/src/**`, replacing them with clear, natural Vietnamese business prose for investors, SME customers, dispatchers, and drivers.
