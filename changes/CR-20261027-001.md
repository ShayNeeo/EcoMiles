# Change Request: CR-20261027-001

## 1. Objective

1. **`User` (Company Logistics Orchestrator — CRM/TMS Left-Sidebar App)**:
   - Defaults to **Fleet & Order Completion Dashboard** (fleet utilization, order completion progress `Đã giao / Đang giao / Chờ giao`, empty-run backhaul recovery, GLEC v3.2 $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$).
   - Left-side CRM/TMS navigation rail switching cleanly between:
     - `Tổng quan Đội xe (Dashboard)`
     - `Điều phối & Thuật toán (Routing & Solver)`
     - `Đơn hàng & Từ điển Cột Excel (Orders & Column Mapper)`
     - `Đội xe & Ghép đơn chiều về (Fleet & Backhaul)`
     - `Báo cáo ESG Tự động (3-Sheet Excel / CSV / PDF)`
     - `Bảo mật & Mã hóa Doanh nghiệp (Zero-Knowledge Privacy)`
2. **`Tester` (EVSIM-Style Dual Side-by-Side HCMC Truck Simulator)**:
   - Adopts the exact visual & simulation architecture from `/home/shayneeo/Downloads/Documents/Coding/V-RIC/EVSIM/web/` (`#bar` top control bar, `#gain` net savings counter, `▶ / ❚❚` clock `06:00–20:00`, speed `×30..×600`, jump buttons `06:00 / 07:30 / 09:05 / 13:15 / 16:30`, `#split` side-by-side synchronized HCMC maps with moving trucks & `★` diff stars, 6 live `.better`/`.worse` KPIs, 76px timeline sparkline canvas, and EVSIM `"Kết quả"` comparison modal with highlighted ROI row).

3. **`Driver` (Auto-Routed Driver Mobile App)**:
   - Interactive HCMC route map + ordered stop cards + 1-tap `Chỉ đường Ô tô (Google Maps travelmode=driving)` + live status writeback (`Đã đến`, `Đã giao`, `Báo hoãn`) + `QĐ 23/2018` ban alert & backhaul pickup card.
4. **`Admin` (Zero-Knowledge System Monitor & User Provisioning Room)**:
   - Strictly blinded to tenant business data: Admin can monitor solver/node telemetry, manage companies & user roles, and inspect the database table where all customer PII and addresses are encrypted via **Client-Side Tenant Envelope Encryption (`AES-256-GCM`)** + **Two-Tier PII Redaction (`privacy-by-design`)**.
