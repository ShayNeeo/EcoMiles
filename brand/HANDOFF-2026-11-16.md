## 8. Known defects and traps

- Mobile card grid: do not drop a paragraph into a two-column card grid without
  explicit areas. Auto-placement put it under the 120 px mark column. Fixed with
  `grid-template-areas:"glyph title" "glyph body"`. Do not regress it.
- Specimen depends on Google Fonts at runtime. Offline it falls back silently.
  Self-host or inline the woff2 before archiving or printing.
- `.display` reports `scrollHeight > clientHeight` because line-height is 1.02.
  That is line-box overflow, not clipping. Do not chase it.
- A vision screenshot reader invented overlaps and misread "trống" as "trông".
  Verify every claim with a DOM rect, never with a screenshot description.
- The first responsive probe ran on about:blank and returned all zeros. Guard with
  `document.querySelectorAll('.markcard').length === 3` before trusting a probe.
- B and C use more of the 64 px box than A, so at equal frame size their ink reads
  slightly smaller. G2 normalises optical size.
- Mono annotations are 10-11 px at 390. Legible, below comfortable phone size.
